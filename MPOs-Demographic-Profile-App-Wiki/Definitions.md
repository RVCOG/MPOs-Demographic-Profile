# Definitions
This section defines the profiles that are used in the Demographic Profile App. These definitions will guide the data standarization for the downloaded ACS Tables.

---

- ***Population:*** is calculated by summing all block group population numbers for a target AOI.

- ***Senior Population:*** is defined as the total population over the age group of 64.

- ***Minority Population:*** is defined as persons who reported their ethnicity and race as a group other than non-Hispanix white-alone [^Minority-Defination] [^Majority-Minority] [^About-Race].

- ***Disability Data:*** Disability data come from the American Community Survey (ACS), the Survey of Income and Program Participation (SIPP), and the Current Population Survey (CPS).[^About-Disability]

- ***Poverty:*** is defined as the population living below the poverty level [^About-Poverty]. ACS Table B17010 contains this data.

- ***Low-Income:*** Low-Income data is the same as ACS Poverty data *- ACS Table C17002 -* and is classified into these three categories [^About-Poverty-Ratio]:  
  - *Severe Poverty:* ratios below **0.50**. 
  - *Below Poverty:* ratios below **1.00** are below the offical poverty definition. 
  - *Near Poverty:* Those with ratios at or above **1.00** but less than **1.25** percent.


- ***Limited English Proficiency (LEP):*** For the purpose of LEP population identification, only those that selected "Not well" or "Not at all" were used to calculate LEP population [^About-Language].
