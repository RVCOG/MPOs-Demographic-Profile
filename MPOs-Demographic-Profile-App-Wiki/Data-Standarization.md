# ACS Data Standarization
This document goes over the process of standarizing ACS data for RVCOG MPOs purposes. Mainly, the data is used for Title VI Plan requirments. During the 2025 Title VI Plan Update for both MPOs, staff is gearing to add an interactive map - **Demographic Viewer Application** - with the ACS data as a component of the Title VI Plan and as a general Demographic Viewer for both MPOs that is used for all kind of other purposes - i.e. Regional Demographic Analysis, Project evaluation and scoring. 



## Why Standarize ACS Table?

The goal of standardizing **American Community Survey (ACS)** tables is to establish a consistent and repeatable data processing workflow. By creating a standardized structure for ACS datasets, each annual or periodic update can be integrated into the system with minimal effort and without requiring the data preparation process to be recreated from scratch.

Standardization ensures that field names, calculated values, demographic indicators, and data relationships remain consistent across all updates. This approach improves data quality, reduces processing time, minimizes the potential for errors, and allows the **Demographic Viewer Application** to consume updated datasets without requiring modifications to the application's configuration, visualizations, or analytical workflows.

Additionally, maintaining a standardized ACS data model supports long-term sustainability by enabling future data updates, documentation, and maintenance activities to be performed more efficiently. This creates a reliable framework for comparing demographic trends over time while ensuring that all demographic profiles and indicators are calculated and presented consistently throughout the application.

By implementing a standardized ACS processing workflow, future data updates become a routine data refresh process rather than a redevelopment effort. This approach reduces maintenance requirements, improves consistency across reporting periods, and ensures that the Demographic Viewer Application remains current with the latest available ACS data while maintaining a consistent user experience.


### Benefits of Standardization

- **Consistency**
  - Ensures all ACS datasets follow the same schema, naming conventions, and calculation methods.

- **Efficiency**
  - Reduces the time required to prepare and publish updated demographic data.

- **Scalability**
  - Simplifies the process of incorporating new ACS releases and additional demographic indicators.

- **Quality Control**
  - Minimizes data processing errors and improves the reliability of analysis results.

- **Application Compatibility**
  - Allows the Demographic Viewer Application to seamlessly consume updated datasets without requiring redevelopment or reconfiguration.

- **Trend Analysis**
  - Supports accurate comparison of demographic characteristics across multiple ACS reporting periods.
